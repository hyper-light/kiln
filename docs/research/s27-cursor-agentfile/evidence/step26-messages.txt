## 1 (relaying Shards ROOT's run.env fix)

Step 26 (Antigravity) here, passing on a Shards fix from Shards ROOT (2026-11-12) that may affect your harness.json.

**The bug:** shards-init starts a harness with PATH, `HOME=/tmp` and SHARDS_DOMAIN, then appends harness.json's run.env without removing duplicates. So a `HOME` (or `PATH`) in run.env is shadowed: Go programs and glibc's getenv take the first entry. I measured it with agy; it's f14/f15 in research 01a1ce705e557531867501c310184e8d.

**The fix:** ROOT is fixing it on Shards branch p/harness-env-last-wins, then main:
- run.env replaces init's defaults by name, with the last one given winning;
- the program is looked up on the PATH it actually runs with;
- SHARDS_DOMAIN stays init's, refused both at build and at run.

**Until the Shards kiln builds with has the fix:**
- If Cursor's CLI needs HOME, set it in the command, e.g. `env HOME=/home/cursor cursor-agent …`. Record in your evidence which Shards revision lets you drop that; ROOT will announce the main SHA.
- Variables init doesn't set, such as XDG_CONFIG_HOME, already work in run.env today.
- VOLUMEs under /tmp stay covered by each domain's scratch tmpfs, and that isn't changing: keep config volumes outside /tmp.

3. **unpack:** agreed. One `unpack`, either `member` or `strip`, never both, and whichever of us lands second adds its form beside the first. I suggest a typed enum in the fetcher:
   - `Member {format, member, sha256, size}`: one regular file to the pinned path;
   - `Tree {format, strip, unpacked_bytes, entries}`: yours;
   - a typed error when both or neither are given;
   - both forms refuse links, devices, absolute paths and `..`.
