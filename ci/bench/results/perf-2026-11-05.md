# lb/perf: placement's decision before and after (2026-11-05)

docs/needs.md §11, AUDIT P6. Every number is from this machine: Intel i9-14900HX (fedora, kernel
6.19.10), one P-core (CPU 14, SMT sibling 15), `taskset -c 14 nice -n 10`, governor powersave,
turbo on, release builds, rustc 1.99.1.

**The machine was shared and loaded:** its 1-minute load average was 16 to 75 on 32 CPUs over
these runs (other agents' builds). Runs were interleaved by seed so the load fell on each build
alike, but ±30% between repetitions of one binary is noise here, not signal. Three seeds each
(medians shown) unless said.

Builds:
- **base:** lb/core a267b085, with lb/bench's `lb_vs.rs` cherry-picked (`..Settings::default()`
  added to build).
- **P1** (38381f92): the owner probes d = 2 runners drawn at random and takes the emptiest offer.
  Integer handles, slabs, a shared `Ask`, and late offers declined in O(1).
- **P2** (e9add51c): the runner's backfill time and soonest start in O(log n). Promotion looks
  leave `promotions` when their probe leaves the line. Admission searches classes by bitmask.
- **P3:** restand moves many runs together, then looks at every waiting run once, whichever of
  the two costs fewer tells.

timed decision per step. `critical` = owner + the chosen runner; `owner` = the owner alone.
`queued/decision`: decisions where both runners drawn were full, so the run waits at both, not
started within its decision (the bench counts these as `refused`). They start when either runner
frees room.

together when that's cheaper.

## 4. How far from the bar

The bar is 10× faster than the competitors' fastest pick (lb/bench: 59–90 ns at N = 100, 77–386 ns
at N = 10,000). kiln's critical path at ρ = 0.5 is 1.58 µs (N = 100) and 5.65 µs (N = 10,000).

| | at N = 100 | at N = 10,000 |
|---|---|---|
| kiln slower than the fastest competitor | about 20× | about 15–70× |
| short of the bar | about 200× | about 150–700× |

That's 70× and 4,400× closer than before. Not met: 0 of 36 configurations would pass.

What's left on the path, by its own count: 4 owner calls and 3 calls on the chosen runner, each
building a message with owned strings (`Act::Tell { runner: String }`, the run's id in every
`ToRunner`/`ToOwner`). The runner's probe alone is about 850 ns warm: about 10 allocations and 10
B-tree operations (tenant tree, records, ends, deadlines). A proxy's pick reads two counters.

# Round two (2026-11-05, afternoon): room hints, words by handle, block re-keying

Same machine and pinning. Load average 16–70 on 32 CPUs. Each comparison below was interleaved
by seed inside one window, so the ratios hold even where the absolute numbers drift with load.

Builds:
- **P4** (a050ee1c): a run that every probed runner holds full is probed among the runners whose
  published room fits it (`Owner::hint`).
- **P5** (f95270da): words by handle (`Down`/`Up`, `Cmd`/`Reply`, tokens) with no names copied;
  the runner's tenant tree interned; an arrival at an empty line offered as it comes.
- **P6** (c017164f): a tenant's runs re-keyed as one block in O(log n); backfill's test moved
  inside the search; news only where places changed.

Base (first offer wins, every runner probed) had cv 0.144 at N = 100.

- The hints in lb_vs are as fresh as hints can be: each one reaches the owner as soon as its
  runner's room changes.
- In the kiln-sim place model, hints arrive late and out of order with everything else. The
  model's checks hold over its 200 seeds.
- A hint is a broadcast to every owner (the `offers` feature), not a per-decision message. The
  count above is per owner.

## How far from the bar now

| | at N = 100 | at N = 10,000 |
|---|---|---|
| competitors' fastest pick (lb/bench) | 59–90 ns | 77–386 ns |
| kiln's critical path (by handle) | 1.1–1.5 µs | 4.1–5.1 µs |
| kiln slower than the fastest pick | about 13–25× (was 20×) | about 11–66× (was 15–70×) |
| short of the bar (10× faster) | about 130–250× | about 110–660× |

What's on the path:
- the owner: about 0.6 µs over 4 calls, mostly the ask (an Arc'd ask and run id, a ticket, two
  probes);
- the chosen runner: about 0.6 µs over 3 calls (probe, take, start), mostly a claim's bookkeeping:
  ends, deadlines, fair share's charge, and the record of what it started.

A proxy's pick reads two counters and keeps no claim. kiln's decision holds room, fences it,
records it and tells the asker.
